InQuestly does not involve trading with real money. Quest Points (QP), coins, points, ranks, the virtual portfolio and badges have no monetary value, are not transferable and cannot be paid out. Manipulating leaderboards or exploiting bugs is not permitted.

6. Price data

For learning purposes, the app shows practice charts and price movements recreated from historical key data. Forecasts are evaluated with daily closing prices that we obtain from Marketstack (currencies: euro reference rates of the ECB); indices, commodities and cryptocurrencies are represented by exchange-traded ETFs or ETCs. The optional live view comes from TradingView and is partly delayed. Price data may be incomplete or incorrect and is not a basis for investment decisions.
