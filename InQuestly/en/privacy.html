InQuestly Privacy Policy

In short: InQuestly works without an account, and we do not operate any servers that hold your data. We do not receive any personal data from the app. The free version shows ads via Google AdMob; with InQuestly Pro the app is ad-free (Section 7).

2. Your learning progress

Learning progress, streak, badges and settings are stored on your iPhone. In addition, the app backs up this state in your own iCloud (Apple iCloud key-value storage) so that it is available again after you switch devices. The data is stored in your Apple Account; we have no access to it. You can turn off iCloud for InQuestly in the iOS settings. You remove the local data with “Delete all data” in the app’s settings or by deleting the app.

3. Leaderboard, friends and camera

If you use the leaderboard, the app transmits your scores (Quest Points in total and this week, forecast points and the result of the weekly challenge) to Apple’s Game Center. When you reach milestones (e.g. first lesson, streaks, certificates), the app also reports the corresponding Game Center achievements to Game Center. Friends and duels work without a server: you share a link, code or QR code yourself (e.g. via a message). It only contains your display name, your avatar, level, streak and forecast rank and – for duels – the chart selection and your result. To scan a QR code, the app uses the camera with your permission; the image is analysed only on your iPhone and is neither stored nor transmitted. Your Game Center profile and Apple’s privacy policy apply there. Use is voluntary; nothing is transmitted without signing in to Game Center. In leaderboards in the app you only see yourself, your Game Center friends and rivals marked as “AI” – never strangers. If someone opens a shared link in the browser instead of in the app, they access this website; the hosting provider then processes the address including the code as described in Section 8.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions, one-time purchases and coin packs are processed by Apple. We do not receive any payment data or any information about you personally; the app merely checks on your device whether a valid purchase exists. Apple provides us with aggregated sales and usage statistics that contain no personal data.

5. Notifications

Reminders about your learning streak and about the deadline of your forecasts are local notifications on your device. When a forecast is due, iOS may briefly launch the app in the background to fetch the closing price via our price service (see Section 6) and to notify you of the result. Only the ticker symbol and the date of the asset are transmitted, no information about you personally. You can turn off notifications in the app or in the iOS settings.

6. Price data, AI and tracking

Price data (price service): For the forecast duel, the app requests daily closing prices from our own price service. It runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA; processing on our behalf, safeguarded by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework). Only the ticker symbol of the asset, the exchange and the period (e.g. “AAPL, Nasdaq, from 1 October”) and – for technical reasons – your IP address are transmitted. The price service does not store IP addresses or user profiles; it merely caches the price series and limits the number of requests per IP address, keeping this in memory for no longer than 24 hours, to prevent abuse. Request logs are not stored. The price service obtains the closing prices from Marketstack (apilayer Data Products GmbH, Vienna, Austria) and exchange rates from the European Central Bank (source: ECB statistics). These providers only receive requests from our price service and no data about you. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (provision of the feature you use). The learning and market charts included in the app are displayed without fetching anything from the internet.

Live price from TradingView (only with your consent): For a forecast, you can view the current price as a chart from TradingView (TradingView, Inc., USA). The chart is only loaded when you tap “Load live price”. Your IP address, the selected ticker symbol and technical information (e.g. browser identifier, language) are then transmitted directly to TradingView; TradingView’s privacy policy applies. The app blocks an analytics pixel from TradingView. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). You can withdraw it at any time under Profile → Settings → “Live prices from TradingView” or directly below the chart (“Hide”); the app then deletes the website data from TradingView stored on your device.

Time check: So that energy, streaks and daily rewards cannot be manipulated by changing the device clock, the app requests the current time from this website (fotiadisapps.de) at launch and every time you return to it. In doing so, the hosting provider only processes the technically necessary access data as described in Section 8 (including your IP address); no content from the app is transmitted. In addition, the app compares the time with your iPhone’s system clock – this happens only on the device. Before you submit a new forecast, the app repeats this check so that nobody can “predict” a price movement that is already known. It also checks a small configuration file on this website at most every 12 hours in case the price service has moved. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a fair game and a working service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

AI: If you phrase a forecast in your own words (“Tesla rises sharply in three months”), Apple’s on-device language model analyses the sentence on your iPhone. No content is sent to AI services, and none is used for training.

No tracking: InQuestly contains no analytics services and does not ask for permission to track you across apps. For advertising in the free version, see Section 7.

7. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of InQuestly is financed by advertising. After completed lessons and exercises, a full-screen ad may appear – never in the middle of a lesson and with a minimum interval between two ads. In addition, you can voluntarily watch short ad clips and receive rewards such as energy or coins in return. There are no banners. With InQuestly Pro you see no ads at all.

Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google AdMob”). Google’s advertising software only starts after the app has been set up, and not while you use InQuestly Pro.

Consent: Before ads are loaded, the app asks you via Google’s consent dialog (User Messaging Platform, IAB TCF) whether Google may use data for personalised advertising. You can change or withdraw your decision at any time in the app under Profile → Settings → “Ad privacy settings”.

Data processed: When loading and displaying ads, Google processes in particular your IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and app information (e.g. model, iOS version, language), a device- or app-related identifier, your interaction with the ad, and diagnostic and performance data. InQuestly does not ask for permission to track you across apps; Google therefore has no access to your iPhone’s advertising identifier (IDFA).

Purposes and legal basis: Delivery, measurement and billing of the ad, fraud prevention and – only with your consent – personalised advertising. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). If you decline, Google may deliver non-personalised ads and only processes the data required for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Transfer to third countries: Google may also process data in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; in addition, the Standard Contractual Clauses of the EU Commission apply.

More information: Google’s privacy policy · How Google uses data from partners’ apps

Children and young people

InQuestly is intended for all age groups. Starting with version 1.0.4, the app asks once for your age group (under 13, 13 to 15, 16 or older). This information stays on your iPhone. If you are younger than 16, the app does not show personalised ads: it then does not ask for consent, and with every ad request Google only receives the instruction to treat it as a request concerning children or young people – without personalisation and without measurement by third parties. All ads in InQuestly are suitable for all age groups. You can change your answer at any time under Profile → Settings → “Age group”. Purchases are handled by Apple; for minors, parents can use “Ask to Buy” in Family Sharing there. The legal basis is compliance with the requirements for the protection of minors (Art. 6(1)(c) and (f) in conjunction with Art. 8 GDPR).

8. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary access data (IP address, time, requested address) in server logs. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; the data may also be processed in the USA. The website does not set cookies and does not use tracking. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

9. Contact by email

If you write to us, we process your email address and your message in order to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them as soon as the request has been dealt with and there is no obligation to retain them.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Since we do not receive any personal data from the app, in practice this only concerns your emails to us. For data that Google processes when displaying ads, you can also exercise your rights directly with Google.
