Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions run via your Apple Account. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → InQuestly → “Cancel Subscription”. In the app, you can also find the way under Profile → Settings → Subscription → “Manage subscription”.

I have a new iPhone – how do I get Pro back?

Open the subscription page in the app and tap “Restore purchases”. Your learning progress comes back automatically from your iCloud if you are signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Profile → gear icon (Settings) → “Data & backup” → “Delete all data”. Since there is no account, we do not hold any of your data. You manage the iCloud backup in the iOS settings under your name → iCloud.

How do I get InQuestly without ads?

With InQuestly Pro the app is completely ad-free – plus unlimited energy and all courses. You can change your consent to personalised advertising in the app under Profile → gear icon (Settings) → “Ad privacy settings”.

How do I add friends?

Profile → “Add friends”: let your friend scan your QR code or scan theirs. Alternatively, share your invitation link via a message. For every new friend there is a coin bonus – for both of you, as soon as you have added each other (up to five friends).

How can I make the text larger?

The learning texts follow your iPhone’s text size: Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size (or Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Larger Text). If “Reduce Motion” is turned on, InQuestly does without animations.

Is InQuestly investment advice?

No. InQuestly is a learning app. Details can be found in the Disclaimer and Risk Notice (in German).
